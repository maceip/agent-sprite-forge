# Scene 4 — Demon (mascot Super Saiyan)

Official clip map: **Clip 4 = this scene.** Background mascot going **Super Saiyan** — power-up / aura outbreak. Keep the anti-kawaii intensity. Lead with Super Saiyan language; a separate rage-demon creature is only an optional FX companion. A RIP tombstone gag may still resolve the beat if it fits.

## Purpose
The **anti-kawaii** emotional outbreak: the **cloud-bot mascot Super Saiyan transformation** / aura blowout that can fill the viewport. Particle illumination should be extreme; this is **not** a cute scene.

Failed render clips show: mascot powers up with a huge aura → flourish/spin → energy burst (optional vanish) → optional tombstone hold. Recreate that interactivity and intensity.

## Style contract
- Same project line art / saturation language, pushed to anime Super Saiyan rage: grit teeth on the face-screen, vein pops, gold/crimson/violet aura, heavy particles, screen-filling illumination.
- Full-viewport allowed — silhouettes can be huge; FX may dominate frame.
- **Primary star = Super Saiyan mascot** (same cloud-bot identity: puffy cloud head + face-screen + stubby limbs), not a generic unrelated demon.
- Tombstone end-card is optional dark comedy, not horror-realistic.
- `#FF00FF` chroma plates. Prefer a mascot-free **aura/particle FX plate** layered over a separate Saiyan-mascot body sheet. Do not kawaii-redesign the power-up.

## Cast & props
1. **Super Saiyan mascot (primary)** — same cloud-bot, now in Super Saiyan outbreak: gold/yellow energy hair-spikes or flame-hair OK, grit-teeth / red-X face-screen, red anger-vein marks, high-contrast lighting, body can grow or stay sticker-scale while aura fills the frame.
2. **Aura / particles** — gold + violet-crimson glow, embers, shock diamonds, screen-filling illumination. This is the blowout.
3. **Optional rage-demon companion** — aggressive horned creature / aura-beast as a **secondary FX companion** to the Saiyan mascot, never a replacement lead. If used, keep it modular and mascot-free on its own plate.
4. **Vanish burst** — energy detonation that clears the pose (optional).
5. **Tombstone** (optional end gag) — black isometric RIP stone, optional spindly hands / purple outline aura for joke horror.
